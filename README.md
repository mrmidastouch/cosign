# Cosign

Cosign, a creator marketplace for TikTok Shop, UGC, and Meta ads, in active development.

## The problem

Brands and creators find each other through scattered DMs and spreadsheets.

## What Cosign is

One marketplace where campaigns are posted, creators apply, work is delivered, and payment is handled.

## Status

Cosign is in active development. The public home is live at join-cosign.com.

## My role

I am the founder, designing the product and the operations around it.

This repo is product and architecture documentation only. It contains no pricing, no internal roadmap, and no technical internals.
